Our Story

A Family Business Built on Love for Animals

Maria and Tom Nguyen founded Evergreen in 2012 in San Antonio, Texas after struggling to find truly natural products for their own pets. Today, every product in the store is vetted for quality, sustainability, and safety.

  • Certified by the Pet Industry Joint Advisory Council (PIJAC)
  • Proud member of the Austin Chamber of Commerce
  • Donate 10% of profits to local animal shelters
  • Carbon-neutral shipping on all orders
